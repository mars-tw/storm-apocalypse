# storm R15 key art style board

## Visual language

- Authored low-poly 3D diorama language matching the shipping R8/R13 cast and world: faceted planes, chunky silhouettes, restrained surface response, no photoreal texture noise.
- Cold whiteout rim light versus the final butcher shop's warm lantern core; foreground characters must remain readable at social-card size.
- Exactly three foreground heroes: coral butcher matron, sage veteran sniper, steel-blue young mechanic. Their role, age/body contrast and equipment silhouette are invariants.
- Essential trio, shop and towers remain inside the central crop-safe region; the left menu-copy zone stays darker and lower-detail.
- No typography, logo, UI, watermark, graphic gore or extra foreground cast inside the bitmap.
